We’ve made a major, systematic improvement to how OpenAlex finds and assigns corresponding authors, and the corresponding institutions tied to them. On a hand-checked gold standard, precision rose from 0.60 to 0.92 while recall held nearly steady (0.91 to 0.88), lifting our F1 score from 0.72 to 0.90.
