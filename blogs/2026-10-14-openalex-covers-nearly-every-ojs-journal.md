We’re excited to announce that 95% of the 55,705 active Open Journal Systems (OJS) journals now have articles in OpenAlex, up from 80%. Over the past month we added 10,870 journals and now harvest 21,864 directly from their own feeds, up from 50. OJS is an open-source publishing platform from the Public Knowledge Project (PKP).
